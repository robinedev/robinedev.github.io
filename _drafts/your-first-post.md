Nothing in `_drafts/` reaches the site, so this file is safe to leave
half finished for as long as you like. To publish it, move it to
`_posts/` and rename it with the date in front, like
`2026-03-14-what-i-learned.md`. The date in the filename is what
Jekyll goes by, so it has to match the one in the front matter above.

Three kinds of post this blog is for, in case the blank page is the
hard part:

**Tech news worth an opinion.** What got announced, what the coverage
skipped, and what it actually changes for anyone running a fleet.

**Whatever is being built right now.** The problem, the first thing
tried, why it didn't hold, and where it ended up.

**Notes from a project or an event.** What you expected going in,
what surprised you, and the one thing worth telling someone going
next year.
