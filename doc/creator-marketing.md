    # Creator & community marketing, for dev tools

    I'm just starting out freelance in SaaS content, and I haven't run
    paid creator deals end to end yet. What I have done is content,
    creative, and community work for [Fleet](https://fleetdm.com),
    an open source device management company, for the same audience, IT
    engineers and developers, that this kind of role is usually trying to
    reach.

    ### Bylined, and fast

    Three published articles on fleetdm.com, including
    [one on the sshole OpenSSH bug](https://fleetdm.com/industry-news/sshhole-openssh-alpine-password-bypass)
    that ran the same week as BleepingComputer and CSO Online, and
    [one on a Microsoft Paint CVE](https://fleetdm.com/industry-news/microsoft-paint-cve-2026-83844-default-apps)
    picked up by Dark Reading and Cybernews. I've also spearheaded Fleet's
    monthly newsletter three editions running:
    [September](https://fleetdm.com/newsletters/newsletter-2026-09),
    [October](https://fleetdm.com/newsletters/newsletter-2026-10), and
    [November](https://fleetdm.com/newsletters/newsletter-2026-11).
    [Everything I've contributed to Fleet](https://github.com/fleetdm/fleet/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Arobinedev)
    is public, if you want the full list instead of the highlights.

    ### a joke that still carries a claim

    fleet's launch posts are usually just release notes. i pitched one as
    a two panel bit instead, a swan in a suit mid "so what did you
    upgrade." [the post](https://lnkd.in/p/eQed9RYR): 13 reactions, 2
    comments, 1 repost, and fleet's own ceo jumped into the comments to
    add more, unprompted. that's the whole bar for a brief. if the founder
    wants to add to it instead of just liking it, it worked.

    also ran [a paid ad](https://www.linkedin.com/ad-library/detail/1572308543)
    built around a soft, dumb visual carrying a real technical hook. 1,000
    to 5,000 impressions, no boost behind it. the joke still has to carry
    a real claim or it's just noise, that part's non negotiable.

    ### Finding the room, not just the feed

    I wrote and posted Fleet's newsletter content straight into the
    MacAdmins Slack, a few thousand IT admins who'd ignore a company
    account. I'm on Reddit most days too, more lurker than poster, which
    is kind of the point: I read what a community actually responds to
    before I pitch anything into it.
