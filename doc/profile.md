# About me

I'm an IT engineer. I work where device management meets identity, and
I like the unglamorous half of the job: a new hire opens a laptop that
already knows who they are, and nobody had to touch it first.

## How I got here

I started on a help desk, with a queue and a stack of laptops. I got
tired of repeating myself and learned to script my way out. That
turned into fleet management, then identity, then the security and
compliance work that pays the bills now.

The thread through all of it: take the thing a human does fifty times
a month and make it happen once, correctly, on its own.

## What I do

- **Endpoints.** Zero touch enrollment, configuration as code, and
  compliance baselines that report on themselves.
- **Identity.** Joiner, mover, and leaver automation, SSO, and access
  that expires without a reminder.
- **Security operations.** Hardening, evidence collection, and turning
  an audit into a report instead of a fire drill.

## What I've shipped

At **TravelPerk** I led IT for North America. When we acquired AmTrav,
I took it from signed deal to 300+ devices live in Intune and Jamf on
day one, and led the IT half of our SOC 2 Type 1.

At **HashiCorp** I ran client platform engineering across 2,000+
devices and 200+ software assets, with automated integrations handling
the asset and compliance tracking people used to chase by hand.

Now I'm head of IT at **FuturHealth**, building the function from
scratch for a growing remote team. I also contribute to **Fleet**, and
got certified at the MacAdmins conference this year.

## Outside the terminal

**Tea, and the foraging behind it.** I've loved tea for as long as I
can remember. My family grew herbs when I was small, and I've since
taken to drying my own foraged flowers and herbs to blend. I try
something new most days.

The foraging goes back to childhood and never stopped. Pawpaw, ramps,
wild onions, and mushrooms, of course.

**Gardening.** The garden's asleep for winter while I plan the next
round. This year: rosemary, pizza thyme, cilantro, and mint, which is
still alive and shows no sign of stopping. Indoors, two dwarf jade
bonsai and a lot of succulents.

**Pickleball.** New to it, but I've played tennis since high school,
so I arrived with opinions about footwork and no idea what the kitchen
was.
