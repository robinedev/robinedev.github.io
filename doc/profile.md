# About me

IT engineer, working where device management meets identity. The
unglamorous half of the job is the good half: a new hire opens a
laptop that already knows who they are, and nobody had to touch it
first.

It started at a managed service provider, running help desk for law
firms. Repeating the same fix got old, scripting turned out to be the
way out, and that grew into fleet management, then identity, then the
security and compliance work that pays the bills now.

## What I do

- **Endpoints.** Zero touch enrollment, configuration as code, and
  compliance baselines that report on themselves. Jamf, Fleet, and
  Rippling MDM.
- **Identity.** Joiner, mover, and leaver automation, SSO, and access
  that expires without a reminder. Okta, Google Workspace, and
  Rippling IdP.
- **Security operations.** Hardening, evidence collection, and turning
  an audit into a report instead of a fire drill. Asset and lifecycle
  tracking in Oomnitza and Iru.
- **Scripting.** Bash and Python, aimed squarely at the jobs nobody
  should do by hand twice. Docker is the one being learned right now.

## What I've shipped

**TravelPerk.** Led IT for North America. The AmTrav acquisition went
from signed deal to 300+ devices live in Intune and Jamf on day one,
alongside the IT half of our SOC 2 Type 1.

**HashiCorp.** Client platform engineering across 2,000+ devices and
200+ software assets, with automated integrations taking over the
tracking people used to chase by hand.

**FuturHealth.** Head of IT now, building the function from scratch
for a growing remote team. Also a **Fleet** contributor, newly
certified at the MacAdmins conference this year.

## Outside the terminal

**Tea, and the foraging behind it.** My family grew herbs when I was
small, and drying my own foraged flowers and herbs to blend came out
of that. Pawpaw, ramps, wild onions, and mushrooms, of course.
Something new goes in the pot most days.

**Gardening.** Asleep for winter while the next round gets planned.
This year: rosemary, pizza thyme, cilantro, and mint, which shows no
sign of stopping. Indoors, two dwarf jade bonsai and a lot of
succulents.

**Pickleball.** New to it, but tennis has been in the mix since high
school, so I arrived with opinions about footwork and no idea what the
kitchen was.
