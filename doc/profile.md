# About me

I'm an IT engineer working where device management meets identity, and
honestly the unglamorous half of the job is the good half. A new hire
opens their laptop, it already knows who they are, and nobody had to
touch it first.

It all started at a managed service provider, running help desk for
law firms. Repeating the same fix got old quickly, scripting turned
out to be the way out, and that grew into fleet management, then
identity, then the security and compliance work that pays the bills
now.

## What I do

- **Endpoints.** Zero touch enrollment, configuration as code, and
  compliance baselines that report on themselves. Jamf, Fleet, and
  Rippling MDM.
- **Identity.** Joiner, mover, and leaver automation, SSO, and access
  that expires without anyone having to remember. Okta, Google
  Workspace, and Rippling IdP.
- **Security operations.** Hardening, evidence collection, and turning
  an audit into a report instead of a fire drill, with asset and
  lifecycle tracking in Oomnitza and Iru.
- **Scripting.** Bash and Python, aimed squarely at the jobs nobody
  should have to do by hand twice. Docker's the one I'm picking up
  right now.

## What I've shipped

**TravelPerk.** I led IT for North America, and when we acquired
AmTrav the migration went from signed deal to 300+ devices live in
Intune and Jamf on day one, with the IT half of our SOC 2 Type 1
shipping right alongside it.

**HashiCorp.** Client platform engineering across 2,000+ devices and
200+ software assets, with automated integrations taking over all the
tracking people used to chase by hand.

**FuturHealth.** I'm head of IT here now, building the function from
scratch for a growing remote team. I contribute to **Fleet** too, and
I got certified at the MacAdmins conference this year!

## Outside the terminal

**Tea, and the foraging behind it.** My family grew herbs when I was
little, and drying my own foraged flowers and herbs to blend came
straight out of that. Pawpaw, ramps, wild onions, and mushrooms of
course! I try something new in the pot most days.

**Gardening.** It's all asleep for winter while I plan the next round.
This year I grew rosemary, pizza thyme, cilantro, and mint, which is
still going strong and shows no sign of stopping. Indoors there's two
dwarf jade bonsai and rather a lot of succulents.

**Pickleball.** I'm new to it, but I've played tennis since high
school, so I turned up with strong opinions about footwork and
absolutely no idea what the kitchen was.
