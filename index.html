Hi, I’m Aube.

I build the IT infrastructure startups run on. Device fleets that set themselves up, identity that grants and revokes itself, and compliance evidence that collects itself while everyone gets on with their day.

I came up through a help desk queue. I got tired of doing the same task fifty times a month, so I learned to script my way out of it. That turned into fleet management, then identity, and then the security and compliance work that keeps auditors calm. Right now I’m head of IT at FuturHealth. Before that, HashiCorp and TravelPerk.

Two things explain how I work. I’m always learning something a little outside my lane, and I’m always holding a cup of tea while I do it. The learning is why I write any of this down. The tea is non-negotiable.

❧

Where I’ve done it

  • FuturHealth Head of IT. I’m building the function from the ground up: identity, endpoints, and access reviews for a remote team.
  • Fleet Contributor. I write about open source device management, and got certified at the MacAdmins conference.
  • TravelPerk Ran IT for North America. I took the AmTrav acquisition from signed to 300+ devices live in Intune and Jamf on day one, and led the IT half of SOC 2 Type 1.
  • HashiCorp Client platform engineering across 2,000+ devices and 200+ software assets. I automated the asset and compliance programs instead of chasing them.
