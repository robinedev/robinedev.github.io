Hi, I’m Aube.

I’m the person who builds the things that get you every application you need on your laptop, keeps your devices and software secure, and makes your everyday workday faster.

I came up through a help desk queue. I got tired of doing the same task fifty times a month, so I learned to script my way out of it. That turned into fleet management, then identity, and then the security and compliance work that keeps auditors calm.

Two things explain how I work. I’m always learning something a little outside my lane, and I’m always holding a cup of tea while I do it. The learning is why I write any of this down. The tea is non-negotiable.
