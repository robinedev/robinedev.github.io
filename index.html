Hi, I’m Aube.

I’m the person who builds the things that get you every application you need on your laptop, keeps your devices and software secure, and makes your everyday workday faster.

I came up from a help desk queue at an MSP for law firms, and these days the job is building the infrastructure underneath it all: endpoints, identity, and the security and compliance work that keeps auditors calm. Doing the same task fifty times a month got old fast, so scripting became the way out, and automating things has been the thread running through everything since. There's always something a little outside my lane that I'm picking up, and there's always a cup of tea going while I do it.
